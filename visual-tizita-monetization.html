Margin guardrails

Rules to protect the business

  • Accept full quality individual uploads but enforce event-level bytes.
  • Never serve originals for ordinary gallery viewing.
  • Control video minutes more aggressively than photo count.
  • Never sell unlimited lifetime storage for a one-time fee.
  • Keep free viewing beautiful; upgrades should add value, not remove frustration.
  • Target >80% gross contribution on normal paid usage.
  • Budget/alert on unusual free-event storage and video spikes.
  • Review actual AWS cost per event monthly.
  • Track free-to-paid conversion by event type.
  • Forecast payroll, support, taxes, marketing, legal and refunds separately from AWS COGS.
