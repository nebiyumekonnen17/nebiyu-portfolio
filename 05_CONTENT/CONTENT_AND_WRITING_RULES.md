# Content and Writing Rules

## Voice

Write like Nebiyu is speaking to a recruiter or engineering manager.

Keep the language direct, specific, calm, and human.

Use concrete facts from the projects.

Avoid generic portfolio language.

Do not use phrases such as:

* cutting edge
* leverage technology
* passionate developer
* innovative solutions
* dynamic professional
* results driven
* seamless experiences
* revolutionize
* transform the future

## Punctuation rule

Do not use em dash in public facing website copy.

Avoid unnecessary hyphens in public facing prose.

Use commas, periods, colons, short sentences, or parentheses instead.

Technology names keep their official spelling when required.

## Honesty rule

Never present a planned system as production.

Never invent numbers.

Never claim a technology was used unless the project data confirms it.

Use status labels consistently:

* Production
* Active Build
* Built
* Development
* Architecture
* Concept

## Recruiter clarity

The homepage must answer these questions quickly:

1. Who is Nebiyu?
2. What kind of engineer is he?
3. What has he actually built?
4. What has he deployed on AWS?
5. What technologies has he used?
6. What credentials has he earned?
7. How can a recruiter view more detail, download a resume, or contact him?

Keep long technical explanations off the homepage.
